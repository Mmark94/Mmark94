### Hi there, I'm Marco 👋

#### Investor & Entrepreneur · Bioinformatician · PhD

I hold a PhD in Bioinformatics and Biotechnology from the University of Manchester and previously worked as a postdoctoral bioinformatician at San Raffaele Hospital in Milan. My research background spans genomics, single-cell RNA sequencing, genome assembly, and computational biology.

Today, my focus is increasingly on investing and entrepreneurship through acquisition. I manage my own investment portfolio and explore opportunities to acquire and develop small and medium-sized businesses, with particular interests in technology, healthcare, and biotechnology.

I still enjoy building things with code. My technical interests include AI, synthetic genome simulation, and testing how AI systems tackle complex bioinformatics problems.

- 🧬 Science & technology: evolutionary biology, synthetic biology, machine learning, and genomics.
- 📈 Business & investing: business acquisitions, LBOs, valuation, and financial modelling.
- ♟️ Beyond work: chess, mathematics, chaos theory, philosophy, and music.
- 💻 Languages: Python · Bash · R · Perl
- 📍 Based in Chieti, Italy.
- 📫 Contact: marcomonti.mm94@gmail.com

I'm happy to connect with people working on interesting scientific problems, building useful software, or exploring investment and business acquisition opportunities.
